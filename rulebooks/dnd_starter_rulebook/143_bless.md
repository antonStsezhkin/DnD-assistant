## Bless 

_1st-level enchantment_ 

Presented in alphabetical order, these spells are used by characters and monsters in this set. 

Some of the spells cause **conditions** , including charmed, deafened, frightened, invisible, paralyzed, prone, restrained, and unconscious. Consult the appendix to learn a condition’s effects in the game.
