## **Components:** V **Duration:** Instantaneous 

Up to six creatures of your choice within range each regain hit points equal to 2d8 + your spellcasting ability modifier. This spell has no effect on undead or constructs. _**At Higher Levels.**_ When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 3rd level or higher, the healing increases by 1d8 for each slot level above 2nd.
