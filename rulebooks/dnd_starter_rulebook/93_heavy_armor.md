## Heavy Armor 

Heavy armor offers the best protection, and it requires extensive training to master. The fighter and the cleric are proficient with it. When you wear heavy armor, you don’t apply your Dexterity modifier to your Armor Class. You also have disadvantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks. 

**Ring mail** is leather with heavy rings sewn into it. **Chain mail** is made of interlocking metal rings worn over a layer of quilted fabric. If your Strength is lower than 13, your speed is reduced by 10 feet while you wear chain mail.

**Splint** armor is made of narrow vertical strips of metal riveted to a backing of leather that is worn over cloth padding. If your Strength is lower than 15, your speed is reduced by 10 feet while you wear splint armor.
