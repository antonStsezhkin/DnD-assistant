## Casting a Spell 

When a character casts any spell, the rules here apply, regardless of the character’s class or the spell’s effects. Each spell description in this chapter begins with a block of information, including the spell’s name, level, school of magic, casting time, range, components, and duration. The rest of a spell entry describes the spell’s effect.
