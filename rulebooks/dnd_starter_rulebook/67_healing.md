## Healing 

Unless it results in death, damage isn’t permanent, and even death is reversible through powerful magic. Rest can restore a creature’s hit points (as explained in chapter 3), and magical methods such as a _cure wounds_ spell or a _potion of healing_ can remove damage in an instant. When a creature receives healing of any kind, hit points regained are added to its current hit points. A creature’s hit points can’t exceed its hit point maximum, so any hit points regained in excess of this number are lost. A creature that has died can’t regain hit points until magic, such as a _revivify_ spell, has restored it to life.
